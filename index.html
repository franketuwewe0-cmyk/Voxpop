📖 What is VoxPop?
VoxPop is a social polling app that gives everyone a stage to be heard. Create polls about anything, vote on what matters, and chat with others who care about the same things.
🎯 Why VoxPop?
Because decisions should be fun, fast, and inclusive. Whether it's a friendly debate, a community vote, or just figuring out where to eat — VoxPop makes every voice count.
✨ What's inside?
🌐 Public & Private Polls
🖼️ Image-based voting
💬 Real-time chats on every poll
⭐ XP & Level system
🔒 Anonymous voting option
🔗 Easy sharing to any app
❤️ Who is it for?
Built for curious minds everywhere — students, friends, communities, and anyone who wants to know what people really think.
Made with ❤️ for people who love to be heard
Help & Support ❓
📝 How do I create a poll?
Tap the yellow ➕ button at the bottom. Type your question, add 2-6 options, then tap "Post Poll".
🔒 What's Private vs Public?
Public polls appear on Explore where anyone can vote.
Private polls only show on your Home.
⭐ How do I earn XP?
+5 XP for voting on a new poll, +2 XP for every chat message, +20 XP for creating a poll. Level up to unlock titles!
👀 How do I see someone's profile?
Tap any username on a poll card. You'll see their bio, level, and public polls.
🔐 I forgot my password!
Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen. Enter your email and we'll send you a reset link.
🔗 How do I share a poll?
On any poll card, tap the blue 🔗 Share button. Choose WhatsApp, Twitter, or Copy the link to paste anywhere.
🗑️ Can I delete my poll?
Yes! On polls you created, tap the red trash can icon next to Share to delete it permanently.
🚪 How do I log out?
Open the menu ☰ (top left) and tap "Logout" at the bottom.
